# Illustrations de la carte Aujourd’hui

Les PNG transparents du widget ont été produits avec ImageGen intégré. La [planche approuvée](../../.agents/skills/jmethod-pencil-butterfly/assets/approved-style-board.png) fournit le papillon de la deuxième colonne. Ces poses servent à la carte du #12.

Les fichiers `widget_{rest,ready,focus,motion,joy}.png` de Compose et les images de même nom dans le catalogue SwiftUI proviennent des mêmes PNG redimensionnés à 512 px au maximum. Le texte reste séparé dans chaque interface.

## Requête de génération

```text
Illustration-story. Single isolated full-body art asset for a small J-Method revision card. Reproduce exactly the pencil butterfly in SECOND COLUMN “2 · Dessiné à la main” of the supplied approved reference. Full-body drawing and happy face in that column only. New pose: {pose}. Three-quarter view, centered, all antenna tips and limbs within generous margin. Slender elongated lilac body, oval pale lavender head, plum expressive eyes and faint pink cheeks, fine curved violet antennae with little elongated yellow-lime tips; four wings behind thorax, larger rounded upper and smaller tapering lower, airy lilac with lime streaks. Delicate hand-drawn irregular plum outlines, translucent colored-pencil hatching and pale internal paper reserves. Preserve character proportions, wing shapes and palette. Genuine transparent background. No words, props, labels, extra limbs, border, shadow, gradients or 3D.
```

| Image | Pose demandée |
|---|---|
| `rest` | hovering at rest with soft eyelids, lowered relaxed arms and partly folded wings |
| `ready` | welcoming the viewer with one small open hand, poised to start flying |
| `focus` | slightly tilted head, small determined smile, one hand near chin, gaze slightly ahead |
| `motion` | flying upward with wing sweep and arms angled gently outward |
| `joy` | joyful upward hop, arms open, eyes closed in happy arcs like the expression reference |
